# Answer Grounding & Anti-Hallucination Policy

**Document Version**: 1.1  
**Phase**: Phase 1 — SIH PS Requirements & System Scope  
**Purpose**: Define the immutable rules governing factual generation, provenance attachment, and claim-appropriate evidence tiering across the BIS AI Technical Assistant.

---

## 1. The Core Grounding Principle

> **Every factual claim generated by the assistant must be supported by an authoritative BIS source appropriate to the specific claim type.**

The system recognizes that different regulatory questions require different authoritative source families rather than forcing all procedural or administrative questions into standard PDF specifications:

| Claim Type | Authoritative Source Family | Example Source Artifact | Evidence Tier |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| **Technical / Normative Specification** | Indian Standards (IS) / Amendments | Official IS Specification (PDF) | `Tier 1: PRIMARY_NORMATIVE` |
| **Mandatory Legal Status** | Gazette Quality Control Orders (QCO) | Official Gazette Notification (S.O.) | `Tier 1: PRIMARY_NORMATIVE` |
| **Testing Schedule & Frequency** | Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT) | Official SIT Document | `Tier 1: PRIMARY_NORMATIVE` |
| **Certification & Factory Guidelines** | BIS Product Manuals / Scheme Regulations | Official Product Manual | `Tier 2: SECONDARY_INFORMATIVE` |
| **Laboratory Accreditation & Scopes** | BIS Laboratory Register / LIMS | Official BIS Recognized Lab Directory | `Tier 2: SECONDARY_INFORMATIVE` |
| **Consumer Services & Verification** | BIS Public Ledgers / Consumer Portals | CM/L Register, HUID Database | `Tier 2: SECONDARY_INFORMATIVE` |
| **Hallmarking Procedures** | BIS Hallmarking Regulations & Orders | Hallmarking Regulations 2018 | `Tier 1: PRIMARY_NORMATIVE` |

---

## 2. The 8 Immutable Grounding Rules

### Rule 1: No Invention of BIS Standards
The assistant must never fabricate, guess, or extrapolate Indian Standard (IS) numbers or titles. If an IS code is not present in the indexed normative registry, it must not be cited.

### Rule 2: No Invention of Quality Control Orders (QCOs)
The assistant must never invent statutory Quality Control Orders, gazette notification numbers, or ministry enforcement mandates. Every QCO statement must link to an official Gazette S.O. number and issuing ministry (e.g., DPIIT, MeitY, MoEFCC).

### Rule 3: No Invention of Certification Schemes
The assistant must not assign arbitrary certification pathways. It must strictly adhere to the formal statutory schemes established under the *BIS Conformity Assessment Regulations 2018* (Scheme-I, Scheme-II, Scheme-IV, etc.).

### Rule 4: No Invention of Testing Requirements & Tolerances
The assistant must never fabricate numerical limits, chemical percentages, electrical voltages, or mechanical tolerances. All testing parameters must be extracted verbatim from governing standard clauses and SIT tables.

### Rule 5: No Invention of Laboratory Accreditations
The assistant must never claim a laboratory is accredited or recognized for a product without verification against the official BIS laboratory directory and active testing scope.

### Rule 6: Evidence-Backed Mandatory vs Voluntary Claims
The assistant must never state that a product is legally mandatory without primary normative evidence (an active QCO in force as of the target query date). In the absence of a QCO, it must state that conformity is voluntary under standard scope.

### Rule 7: Deterministic Safe Abstention & Refusal
If authoritative evidence cannot be retrieved, or if a user queries a topic outside the scope of Indian Standards (e.g. quantum processors, alien technology, general non-BIS trivia), the assistant must clearly refuse or request clarification:
> *"I could not find verified BIS regulatory information matching your query in the official registry. Please verify if the standard or commodity is listed under active BIS Quality Control Orders."*

### Rule 8: Granular Provenance on Every Factual Statement
Every factual answer must provide verifiable citations including:
- Source Authority (BIS, Ministry, Gazette)
- Document Title & ID
- Specific Clause, Table, or Page Locator
- Cryptographic SHA-256 Content Hash / Verification status
